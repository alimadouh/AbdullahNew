# Audit: batches AD and AE

Batch AD: desmopressin, cabergoline, tamsulosin, denosumab, allopurinol, febuxostat.
Batch AE: progesterone, norethisterone, dydrogesterone, estriol-lactobacillus, prednisolone, dexamethasone.

This is an independent second check, done on 2026-10-17. All 35 sources in `sources/AD.json` and `sources/AE.json` were read in full with `fetchtext.mjs --all`. For every UK-sourced medicine, SmPC sections 4.1 to 4.6 and 4.8 were read. The 4.8 frequency tables were also read from the raw emc HTML, because the flattened text loses the table columns. emc search and the MHRA products search index (the index behind products.mhra.gov.uk) were queried again for dydrogesterone, the estriol/lactobacillus trade name, lactobacillus, estriol and norethisterone 10 mg.

After the edits, every quote in the 12 medicine files and their group memberships was re-checked against the cached page text with `lib/text.mjs` `quoteFound`. The result was **1285 quotes checked, 0 not found**. `validate()` reports 0 schema errors in total. A test build to the scratchpad (not to `public/`) and engine runs confirmed the new rules fire as planned (see section 3).

## 2. Coordinator questions, answered

- **Dydrogesterone: kept as a UK product.** The source is not Duphaston. It is Nalvee 10 mg film-coated tablets (Gedeon Richter), PL 04854/0198, first authorised 21/06/2024, text revised 07/08/2026, and listed on emc as a current product (updated 17 Aug 2026). The MHRA products index also lists Dydrogesterone Waymade 10 mg (PL 06464/3141, SPC May 2026). The active substance, salt, route and strength (10 mg oral tablet) match the clinic product.
- **Estriol-lactobacillus: no-uk-product confirmed.** emc returns "No search results" for the trade name and for "lactobacillus". The MHRA products index has 0 hits for the trade name. Every "estriol" and "lactobacillus" hit (for example the estriol 0.03 mg pessary) has substance_name ESTRIOL only. The file has `rules: []`, every section is not-available, and the formulation has no `src`.
- **Desmopressin enuresis-only contraindications.** Before this check, these were qualified: blood pressure (both rules) and age over 65. They are now also qualified: hyponatraemia and SIADH. Kept for all uses: heart failure, the diuretic-condition rule, psychogenic polydipsia and alcohol abuse (both indication lists name them), and hypersensitivity. Renal CrCl < 50 is also kept for all uses, with the reason given in section 1.
- **Allopurinol renal as conditions.** Agreed. Zyloric 4.2 gives only "severe renal insufficiency" and the dialysis schedule, with no CrCl/eGFR numbers. No change.
- **Strength mismatches.** Norethisterone (10 mg clinic vs 5 mg SmPC), prednisolone syrup (15 mg/5 mL vs 10 mg/mL) and dexamethasone syrup (0.5 mg/5 mL vs 2 mg/5 mL): every dosing `t` is written in mg, never in tablets or mL. Each formulation note now explains the conversion. No UK 10 mg norethisterone tablet was found on emc (5 mg only) or in the MHRA products index.
- **Progesterone and other vaginal products.** The source wording ("concomitant vaginal products") supports all of them. Rules use exact ids plus `otherFormulations` with the vaginal formulation ids only. A test build confirmed: clotrimazole pessary → avoid; clotrimazole cream → nothing; clindamycin capsules → nothing.
- **Corticosteroid group.** `corticosteroid` = prednisolone (oral tablets and solution) and dexamethasone (oral solutions) only, each sourced from its own SmPC 5.1. The ear/eye steroid combinations are not members, and the engine ignores ingredient matches for local routes. A test build confirmed: denosumab + prednisolone ear drops → nothing.

## 4. Problems not fixed (for other batches or the integrator)

1. **Engine `isLocal` and progesterone pessaries.** The progesterone formulation route is "vaginal or rectal", which matches the local-route regex. As a result, other medicines' rules toward `group:progestogen` are ignored for the clinic pessary: `carbamazepine:interaction:progestogen`, `furosemide:interaction:progestogen`, `glimepiride:interaction:progestogen` and `insulin-glargine:interaction:progestogen`. But 400 mg pessaries are used for systemic effect (luteal support), and the progesterone SmPC itself lists carbamazepine as lowering bioavailability. The integrator should decide whether progesterone pessaries need a systemic exception. Progesterone's own rules still fire.
2. **Groups missing on combination antihypertensives (batches outside AD/AE).** `amlodipine-valsartan-hydrochlorothiazide` and `telmisartan-amlodipine` have `groups: []`. So the antihypertensive, diuretic and thiazide rules in this audit (cabergoline, tamsulosin, allopurinol, desmopressin diuretic-condition, prednisolone, dexamethasone) do not fire for them, while the two-drug versions do fire.
3. **Desmopressin diuretic-condition rule.** SmPC 4.3 contraindicates "conditions requiring treatment with diuretic agents". It is encoded as `contraindicated` toward `group:diuretic`, which includes thiazide combinations used only for hypertension. This is a literal reading. The integrator may prefer a condition-based rule.
4. **Not mapped (text only):** cabergoline + antipsychotics (none in the master list); allopurinol/febuxostat + azathioprine/mercaptopurine (not in the list); dexamethasone as a moderate CYP3A4 inducer lowering CYP3A4 substrates (no substrate group); prednisolone/dexamethasone + oestrogens (only the no-UK estriol product); tamsulosin "do not combine in CYP2D6 poor metabolisers" (genotype cannot be encoded; it is in the action text).
5. **Dexamethasone and prednisolone excipient rules** (hereditary fructose intolerance; lactose in tablets) come from UK products. The clinic syrups may have different excipients. The notes say so, but the dexamethasone HFI rule still fires for both clinic syrups.

## 5. Per-medicine assessment

- **Desmopressin: good after the fixes.** The two UK 120 microgram lyophilisate SmPCs match the clinic melt. 4.3 is fully encoded and the enuresis-only items are now labelled. The interactions (TCA, SSRI, carbamazepine, NSAIDs: monitor; loperamide: caution; sulfonylureas: info) match 4.4/4.5. The adverse-frequency error is fixed.
- **Cabergoline: good, no changes.** The Dostinex 0.5 mg SmPC is current. 4.3 is complete: ergot allergy, fibrosis, hepatic insufficiency, toxaemia, puerperal psychosis, long-term valvulopathy (qualified). The 4.5 rules (dopamine antagonists, macrolides: avoid) and the 4.8 frequencies were checked against the HTML table.
- **Tamsulosin: good.** The clinic 0.4 mg tablet matches the UK 400 microgram prolonged-release tablet. 4.3 is complete. The CrCl < 10 caution is correct. The moderate-inhibitor rule no longer double-fires for clarithromycin.
- **Denosumab: good.** The 60 mg product only; the note says the 120 mg product differs. Hypocalcaemia contraindication, under-18 avoid (SmPC + MHRA 2022), CrCl < 30 monitoring (SmPC + MHRA 2014) and MHRA 2020 stopping advice are all present. The glucocorticoid rule was added.
- **Allopurinol: good.** HLA-B*5801 rules, renal condition rules (no numbers in the SmPC), and the warfarin, amoxicillin, diuretic, ACE inhibitor and aluminium hydroxide rules match 4.4/4.5. The SmPC vs BUMPS pregnancy difference is shown. The salicylate rule is now dose-limited.
- **Febuxostat: good, no changes.** MHRA 2023 cardiovascular advice is encoded as caution, with the NICE allopurinol-first advice. The 4.8 "common" list matches the table. The allopurinol cross-hypersensitivity rule comes from MHRA 2012.
- **Progesterone: good after the fixes.** 4.3 is complete. The vaginal-product rule now covers all vaginal products in the master list. The frequencies are corrected. See problem 1 (isLocal).
- **Norethisterone: good after the fixes.** Two UK 5 mg SmPCs with different 4.3 lists are kept separate and labelled. The strength note is clarified, the over-firing macrolide rule is narrowed, and 3 condition rules were added.
- **Dydrogesterone: good.** A current UK licence (Nalvee, 2024). 4.3 is complete. The "not authorised in pregnancy" info and the breastfeeding "should not" (avoid) match 4.6. One monitor rule was added.
- **Estriol-lactobacillus: correct as no-uk-product.**
- **Prednisolone: good.** Formulation-scoped contraindications follow the three UK SmPCs (5 mg tablets, 20 mg tablets, 10 mg/mL solution). The 4.5 rules (CYP3A inhibitors avoid, inducers dose, antidiabetics dose, anticoagulants and potassium-losing drugs monitor) match the sources. The syrup note is clarified.
- **Dexamethasone: good.** Both UK 2 mg/5 mL SmPCs are used, and their differences (4.3, breastfeeding) are kept as separate rules. SPS and BUMPS are quoted. The 0.5 mg/5 mL note is clarified.
