# Audit: batches AJ, AK and AL

Independent second check of the clinic-expansion batches AJ, AK and AL, 2026-10-17.

I read in full sections 2 and 4.1 to 4.9 of every SmPC used: the two ichthammol and permethrin MHRA SPCs, emc-4860, emc-4901, emc-4218, emc-6552, the six chloramphenicol SmPCs (emc-9770, emc-7299, emc-427, emc-9662, emc-13182, emc-5195), emc-841, emc-842, emc-529, emc-66, emc-3347, emc-102502, the 2026 MHRA choline salicylate SPC and emc-624. I also read the SPS, BUMPS and MHRA pages these batches cite, and the clinic map rows for all 20 medicines.

After the edits:
- all 627 quotes in the 20 medicine files and the AJ/AK/AL group members are FOUND (lib/text.mjs `quoteFound`, the same test as `--check`);
- `validate()` reports 0 schema errors in the whole KB;
- a scan of label, strengths, note, t, tag, concern, consequence, action, className and name finds no brand, manufacturer or emc id (only the site name "emc" in search notes);
- every formulation route is local (topical, eye, ear or oromucosal), except the anti-haemorrhoid suppository (`rectal`, see "Problems not fixed").

**Medicines converted to no-uk-product:** dexpanthenol-chlorhexidine.

**No-uk-product confirmed (kept):** dexpanthenol, beta-sitosterol-beeswax, anti-haemorrhoid, neomycin-polymyxin-prednisolone-ear, gentamicin-dexamethasone-ear, ciprofloxacin-hydrocortisone-ear, phenol-glycerol-ear, naphazoline-pheniramine, polyvinyl-alcohol-povidone, krameria. A fresh emc search gave "No search results" for pheniramine, polyvinyl alcohol, krameria, ciprofloxacin hydrocortisone, gentamicin dexamethasone and prednisolone neomycin. "naphazoline" goes straight to the naphazoline + witch hazel product. "dexpanthenol" finds only an intravenous multivitamin, a nasal spray and a vitamin B syrup. "phenol" finds only an antiseptic cream, a paste and oily phenol injection.

**Checked and left unchanged (correct):**
- **Ichthammol:** the only UK single-ingredient product is ichthammol 10% in glycerol (PL 18374/0020). It has the same active, and the note says the form differs. The 4.3 allergy rule is `contraindicated`. Broken skin is `avoid` ("Do not apply"). Pregnancy is `caution` (benefit-risk).
- **Permethrin:** the 1% creme rinse and 1% cutaneous emulsion are the same active and strength as the clinic shampoo, and the note says the form differs. The 4.3 pyrethroid/pyrethrin allergy rule is `contraindicated`. Compositae allergy is `avoid` ("only if strictly indicated"). Pregnancy is `avoid`. Breastfeeding is `caution` ("consideration should be given"). The three age rules match each SPC's own limit. 4.5 says "None known" / "No adverse effects reported".
- **Dimeticone (lice):** the 4% lotion (two SmPCs) is the same active. The fire risk is from the SmPC and the MHRA DSU 2018. Under 6 months is `caution` (medical supervision).
- **Gentamicin drops:** the 4.3 items (allergy, other-aminoglycoside allergy, myasthenia gravis, perforated eardrum scoped to ear use) are `contraindicated`. The mtDNA variant rule is `caution` (MHRA DSU 2021). Contact lens and dry eye rules are scoped to eye use. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are `caution` ("only when considered essential"). The eye ointment has no UK SmPC; the drops SmPCs are used with a clear note, and drop-only items are scoped away from it.
- **Chloramphenicol eye:** all 4.3 items were checked against all six SmPCs. The differences (personal history of blood dyscrasia missing from emc-7299/5195; previous myelosuppression missing from emc-13182) are named in the concerns. Bone-marrow-depressant drugs are `avoid`. The newborn dose rule is scoped to drops.
- **Neomycin/polymyxin/dexamethasone eye:** all 4.3 items are rules. CYP3A4 inhibitors are `avoid`; the SmPC says "CYP3A4 inhibitors", not "potent", so `group:cyp3a4-inhibitor` matches the wording. Antidiabetic and diabetes rules are `caution`. The child intraocular pressure rule is `monitor`.
- **Chlorhexidine mouthwash, benzydamine:** checked against both SmPCs each. The levels match the wording (under-12 `avoid`; under-13 `avoid`; aspirin/NSAID hypersensitivity `avoid` ("not advisable"); asthma `caution`; pregnancy and breastfeeding `avoid` ("should not be used unless")).
- **Choline salicylate:** under-16 is `contraindicated` (SPC 4.3 + MHRA/CHM). The allergy and NSAID-hypersensitivity rules are `contraindicated`. Breastfeeding is `contraindicated` (2026 SPC 4.6 says "contraindicated"). Aspirin is `avoid`. Anticoagulants are `info` (no action stated). It is not in the `salicylate` or `nsaid` group (local product).
- **Group memberships:** `pyrethroid` (permethrin) is sourced from both SPCs 5.1. `bone-marrow-depressant` (methotrexate, carbamazepine) is sourced from their own SmPCs. No local product is in a systemic group.

## 2. Problems not fixed

1. **Engine: duplicate-ingredient check ignores route.** gentamicin-dexamethasone-ear (dexamethasone), ciprofloxacin-hydrocortisone-ear (ciprofloxacin, hydrocortisone), neomycin-polymyxin-prednisolone-ear (prednisolone) and neomycin-polymyxin-dexamethasone-eye (dexamethasone) share ingredient ids with oral medicines. The tool will flag "same active ingredient" (caution) when an ear or eye product is given with the oral one. This is for the integrator, in `engine.js`.
2. **Anti-haemorrhoid suppository route `rectal`** is not in the engine's `LOCAL_ROUTE` list. It is harmless now (no-uk-product, no rules, no groups, unique ingredient id), but the integrator should decide whether rectal products count as local.
3. **Empty groups.** `ototoxic` and `aminoglycoside` have no members, so the gentamicin and neomycin rules that point at them are inert. `nephrotoxic` has ibuprofen, naproxen and methotrexate, so the two `avoid` nephrotoxic rules on the eye/ear products fire with those oral products. The level is kept at source strength, and the consequence now carries the low-absorption context. The integrator may want to review this as a policy question.
4. **`bone-marrow-depressant`** is narrow (methotrexate, carbamazepine). Widening it needs sourced memberships from other batches.
5. **Clinic data mismatch:** the gentamicin-dexamethasone-ear row gives a trade name and link for a UK neomycin/polymyxin B/hydrocortisone product. The clinic entry should be checked.
6. **Not added:** the permethrin emulsion excipient warning ("babies less than 4 weeks old with open wounds…") is specific to the UK product's propylene glycol, and the clinic shampoo's excipients are unknown. The choline salicylate "other salicylates" wording could also cover the topical salicylate rubs; no topical-to-oral-gel rule was made. The naphazoline-only UK eye drops were not used as a stand-in for the combination.
7. **Licence status:** I did not separately confirm that every product is still marketed. The MHRA documents are current index entries (ichthammol indexed Jul 2026, creme rinse May 2024, emulsion Nov 2021), and the emc pages are live and not marked discontinued.
