Second, independent check of the batch D data, done on 2026-10-17. Every section 4.1 to 4.9 of each SmPC/THR SPC listed in `sources/D.json` was re-read in full from `fetchtext --all` output. Every new or changed quote was checked with `--check` and found. `verify.mjs` was run after the changes and reported 4468/4468 quotes found, 0 schema errors and 0 warnings. `test-engine.mjs` passed (27 tests). The changed rules were also run through `assess()` in a scratch script to confirm that they fire as intended.

**Checked and found correct (no change):**
- **Metformin renal table.** The SmPC uses "GFR (mL/min)", so the measure `GFR` is right. Thresholds are <30 contraindicated, 30–44 max 1000 mg, 45–59 max 2000 mg, and 60–89 max 3000 mg (IR) or 2000 mg (SR). The SR maximum is correctly limited by formulation. I checked the emc HTML: the "Additional considerations" cell has `ROWSPAN="2"`, so "review lactic acidosis risk factors" and "starting dose at most half the maximum" apply to **both** 45–59 and 30–44. The 30–44 rule is therefore right.
- **Levothyroxine tablet vs oral solution 4.3.** The tablet SmPC lists thyrotoxicosis and "adrenal gland disorder or adrenal insufficiency". The oral solution lists "adrenal insufficiency without adequate corticosteroid cover", and its 4.4 says "should not be given" in hyperthyroid states. The per-formulation rules (`contraindicated` / `avoid` / `contraindicated`) are faithful.
- **Omeprazole + clopidogrel = `avoid`.** The SmPC says "should be discouraged", MHRA says "discouraged unless considered essential" and SPS says "Avoid". Mapped to avoid, which is correct.
- **St John's wort + warfarin = `contraindicated`.** Both THR SPCs list it in 4.3, so this is correct.
- **St John's wort + clopidogrel.** Clopidogrel is not in either THR table, so correctly there is no rule. **St John's wort + metformin:** only gliclazide is named, so correctly there is no rule.
- **Metformin + trimethoprim (OCT2).** The rule also fires for co-trimoxazole through its `trimethoprim` ingredient. The engine test confirmed this. The original note saying "no co-trimoxazole rule" is therefore moot.

## 2. Problems found but not fixable here

1. **Levothyroxine: anti-thyroid drug combination in pregnancy.** Both SmPCs list this in 4.3. No anti-thyroid drug is in the master list, and a pregnancy rule would fire for every pregnant woman on levothyroxine, so it stays as a table item only.
2. **Levothyroxine: oestrogens/oral contraceptives may raise dose needs.** The available condition `hormonal-contraception` also covers progestogen-only implants and injections, which the SmPC does not name. A rule would over-fire, so this is a table item only. The same applies to the aspirin/NSAID false-low thyroid hormone results, which are a laboratory interference and not a prescribing interaction.
3. **St John's wort with antiepileptics.** Kira contraindicates this and MHRA says "not recommended". There are no antiepileptics in the master list. The `epilepsy` condition rule stays `avoid` (MHRA wording), because having epilepsy is not the same as taking a specific antiepileptic.
4. **Sertraline contraindications that cannot be expressed.** MAOIs, pimozide, linezolid, moclobemide and disulfiram (concentrate only) are not in the master list. Sertraline + alcohol ("not recommended") has no matching condition: `alcohol-dependence` is narrower than the statement.
5. **Metformin.** "Any acute metabolic acidosis" can only be mapped to the `diabetic-ketoacidosis` condition. Glucocorticoid, sympathomimetic, insulin and sulfonylurea interactions have no master-list members (`group:corticosteroid` is empty).
6. **Excipient intolerances.** Lactose (Eltroxin, Losec capsules, Kira), sucrose (Losec MUPS) and parahydroxybenzoates (levothyroxine solution) have no matching vocab condition, so they are table items only.
7. **Other batches (outside my edit scope; flagged for their auditors):**
   - **Batch F.** `methotrexate:interaction:ppi` is `info` (SPS). However, Methotrexate 2.5 mg SmPC emc-511 says "Concomitant use of proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) and high dose methotrexate should be avoided, especially in patients with renal impairment."
   - **Batches E, F and B.** `group:oral-antidiabetic` currently contains only metformin. The ciprofloxacin, clarithromycin, hydroxychloroquine and lisinopril rules that use it now flag metformin. Those SmPC statements are mostly about hypoglycaemia with sulfonylureas or insulin, so those batches should confirm that metformin is meant.
   - **Batch C.** The Plavix SmPC raises bleeding caution with "CYP2C19 strong inducers". No UK source I read calls St John's wort a *strong* CYP2C19 inducer, so I made no rule.
8. **Engine limitation.** Metformin uses `measure: "GFR"`, so every metformin renal finding is shown as provisional ("the source says GFR"). This is correct behaviour, but it is noisy.

## 3. Overall assessment

- **Metformin.** Accurate and close to complete for the two Glucophage SmPCs. All 4.3 contraindications are present as rules. The renal thresholds, the IR vs SR maximum doses and the IR/SR paediatric differences are right. My fixes were small sourcing gaps. The surgery and contrast rules are `avoid`, a deliberate and documented choice.
- **Levothyroxine.** Accurate. The tablet vs oral solution 4.3 differences are handled correctly. My changes were one missing age rule (tablets under 5) and several unsourced or under-sourced statements. Renal and hepatic are correctly "not-stated". This data is based on only one tablet SmPC (Eltroxin 100 micrograms) and one oral-solution SmPC; other generics were not reviewed.
- **Omeprazole.** Accurate. Clopidogrel, methotrexate, warfarin and St John's wort are covered, and the omeprazole–clopidogrel level is right. My changes added an SPS breastfeeding source the original missed and removed two unsourced statements. The interaction coverage on omeprazole's own side is thin (several rules are `info` because the SmPC states no action), but this is faithful to the source.
- **Sertraline.** Mostly accurate, but there was one real level error: the group serotonergic rule (in effect sertraline + tramadol) was `avoid` where the SmPC says caution. One rule also merged SmPC and BUMPS advice. The bleeding, QT, hyponatraemia and hepatic rules are faithful. The QTc evidence conflict between SPS and the SmPC is now recorded. This data rests on one tablet SmPC (Lustral 50 mg) plus the Rosemont concentrate.
- **St John's wort.** This had the most significant completeness gap. The second THR SPC (Kira) treats its whole interaction table as 4.3 contraindications, and also contraindicates peanut/soya allergy, but neither fired as a rule. Both are now added, limited to that product. The Hyperiforce-based `avoid` rules are faithful to "should not be taken concomitantly". The level depends on the product, because THR SPCs differ. Users choosing an unregistered St John's wort product get the Hyperiforce (`avoid`) behaviour by default.
