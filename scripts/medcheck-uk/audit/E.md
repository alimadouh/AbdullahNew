## Flagged items checked and left as they were (with reasons)

- **Clarithromycin + simvastatin**: contraindicated. Correct (sections 4.3 and 4.5, and SPS).
- **Clarithromycin + atorvastatin**: SPS "Avoid … maximum 20mg daily if unavoidable" and MHRA "do not exceed 20 mg" are kept as an `avoid` rule. The SmPC's weaker "caution with other statins" stays as a separate group rule. They merge into one row at `avoid`. The SPS and MHRA quotes were re-read in context. Correct.
- **QT and electrolytes**: clarithromycin with history of QT prolongation, hypokalaemia or hypomagnesaemia is contraindicated (section 4.3 says "should not be given", section 4.4 says "contraindicated"). Ciprofloxacin is caution only ("uncorrected" electrolyte imbalance). Both correct. The `qt-prolonging` group holds clarithromycin, ciprofloxacin and hydroxychloroquine. SPS says sertraline has no QTc effect at licensed doses, so it was correctly not added.
- **Clarithromycin's other 4.3 co-administrations** (ergot alkaloids, oral midazolam, lomitapide, ticagrelor, ivabradine, ranolazine, colchicine, astemizole, cisapride, domperidone, pimozide, terfenadine, lovastatin): none is on the master list. They are listed in the section only, which is correct.
- **Ciprofloxacin and polyvalent cations**: the separate rules for iron, calcium, magnesium and antacid groups are correct ("1-2 hours before or at least 4 hours after"). Level `caution` is a reasonable mapping for a timing instruction.
- **Ciprofloxacin and NSAIDs (seizures)**: not stated in either ciprofloxacin SmPC, so correctly absent. The ibuprofen and naproxen files carry it from their own SmPCs.
- **Ciprofloxacin and methotrexate** is `avoid` ("not recommended"). **Warfarin** is `monitor`. **Clopidogrel** is on the clopidogrel side (MHRA CYP2C19). All correct.
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding levels**: SmPC and BUMPS/SPS are kept as separate rules with the right strengths. One exception was fixed in #14.

## 2. Problems found but not fixable (data files only)

1. **Engine: no AND across inputs.** The clarithromycin "severe hepatic failure + renal impairment" contraindication fires on severe hepatic impairment alone. Wording now explains this (#11). A proper fix needs an engine extra-limit for renal function on hepatic rules.
2. **Engine: ingredient-level rule matching.** Any rule whose `med` is an ingredient id applies to every combination product with that ingredient. This was fixed for amoxicillin by limiting formulations (#2). The integrator should check other batches for the same effect: co-codamol picks up codeine and paracetamol rules, and co-trimoxazole picks up trimethoprim rules. Allergy tokens have a related effect: `group:penicillin` expands to co-amoxiclav's ingredients, so a recorded allergy to clavulanic acid alone triggers amoxicillin's penicillin contraindication. That is a cautious over-flag.
3. **Engine: min is always inclusive.** For ciprofloxacin tablets, CrCl exactly 30 shows both the "≤30" (24-hourly) rule and the "30-60" (12-hourly) rule. The 24-hourly one is correct for tablets. The overlap is harmless but noisy.
4. **No weight-based rules.** These limits cannot be encoded:
   - co-amoxiclav tablets "must not" be used in children < 25 kg;
   - 250/125 tablets are not recommended in children < 40 kg;
   - the 2:1 renal restriction in children < 40 kg;
   - the suspension "NR" cells.

   All of these remain in the section text only.
5. **Co-amoxiclav suspension "NR" table** (flagged by the researcher as an age table). It is in fact an embedded image (SMPC_19187_image1_20.png on emc 278). It gives dose volumes by body weight (2 to 39 kg); for the 125/31 suspension the 60 mg/15 mg/kg/day column is "NR" up to 11 kg. No text in the page carries it, so it cannot be quoted or verified. Not encoded.
6. **Clarithromycin + oral antidiabetics → metformin.** The SmPC says "oral hypoglycaemic agents (such as sulfonylurias) and/or insulin". The only master-list member of `oral-antidiabetic` is metformin, and metformin's own SmPC (emc 987) says it "does not cause hypoglycaemia" alone. The `monitor` rule therefore fires for metformin on a literal reading of the class. It was kept because the source names the class, but it is probably an over-flag. The same applies to ciprofloxacin, whose wording is closer: dysglycaemia "usually in diabetic patients receiving … an oral hypoglycaemic agent".
7. **Suspension renal thresholds in ml/min/1.73 m²** are labelled "creatinine clearance" by the source. They are encoded as `CrCl`, following SCHEMA. Clarithromycin suspension and both ciprofloxacin SmPCs are affected. The engine compares them with Cockcroft-Gault CrCl in mL/min, which is not normalised for body surface. This matters most in children.
8. **Other ciprofloxacin risk factors not encoded** (no condition ids): Turner syndrome, Behçet's disease, Takayasu or giant cell arteritis, Sjögren's syndrome and known atherosclerosis. Mapping atherosclerosis onto ischaemic heart disease and similar ids would be inference. These remain in the cautions text.
9. **No SPS quinolone breastfeeding page.** Re-checked: several candidate URLs return 404. Ciprofloxacin breastfeeding rests on the SmPCs only.
10. **Condition overlap.** The batch E condition `hereditary-fructose-intolerance` and the batch G condition `hereditary-sugar-intolerance` overlap. Sucrose-containing suspensions now carry a rule for each. The integrator may want to merge the two conditions.

## 3. Overall assessment

- **Amoxicillin.** Accurate against emc 526/14146. Indications, dosing tables, paediatric mg/kg, contraindications and interactions match. The renal band at exactly 30 was wrong. The cross-firing onto co-amoxiclav was a real scope error. Both are fixed. Completeness is good for the master-list interactions (warfarin, methotrexate).
- **Co-amoxiclav.** Accurate across the six SmPCs, with the formulation-specific renal handling (2:1, 4:1 and 7:1) correctly separated. One boundary error and one missing excipient rule were fixed. Weight-based restrictions are the main remaining gap, and they are an engine limit. The suspension NR table is unencodable.
- **Clarithromycin.** The interaction set is accurate and complete for the master list (simvastatin contraindicated, atorvastatin avoid, warfarin, antidiabetics, amlodipine, sildenafil, hydroxychloroquine, QT, St John's wort, omeprazole). Section 4.3 condition contraindications are correctly levelled. Fixed:
  - the XL renal boundary;
  - the SPS breastfeeding level;
  - the missing renal-impairment caution and excipient rules.

  The hepatic-plus-renal rule over-fires by design limitation.
- **Ciprofloxacin.** Accurate and thorough on MHRA restrictions, aortic/valve risk, tendon, psychiatric and QT warnings, and cation separation. The renal encoding was wrong at both boundaries (30 for tablets, 60 for both) and is now formulation-specific. Missing risk-factor rules were added: renal impairment (tendon), infective endocarditis, rheumatoid arthritis, sugar intolerance.

No number, dose or unit errors were found in the section text beyond the renal boundaries above. I did not re-derive every row of the dosing tables independently beyond reading them against the source text. The paediatric weight-band tables for the ciprofloxacin and co-amoxiclav suspensions were not encoded as `calc` blocks and were not checked in detail.
