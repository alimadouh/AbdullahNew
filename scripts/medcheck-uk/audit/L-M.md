# Audit: batches L and M

This is an independent second check, done on 2026-10-17. It covers 13 medicines:

- **Batch L:** amlodipine-valsartan, amlodipine-valsartan-hydrochlorothiazide, perindopril, perindopril-amlodipine, lisinopril-hydrochlorothiazide, captopril.
- **Batch M:** indapamide, hydrochlorothiazide, furosemide, co-amilozide, atenolol-bendroflumethiazide (built as atenolol/chlortalidone), methyldopa, moxonidine.

**How it was checked**
- Every cited source was re-read in full from the shared cache (SmPC 4.1 to 4.9).
- Every rule and item was checked against the brief: product, faithfulness, level, numbers, interactions, groups, completeness and generic-only display text.

**Final state**
- All 2,372 quotes in these 13 files and in the L/M group-member entries are FOUND.
- The schema check shows 0 errors over the whole KB, and no warnings for these ids.
- `test-engine.mjs` passes 38/38 tests and `test-scenarios.mjs` passes 54/54.
- No brand, manufacturer or emc id appears in any display field.

**Level policy used**
- SmPC 4.3 → contraindicated.
- "should not", "not recommended" and "avoid" outside 4.3 → avoid.
- An interaction that states an effect but gives no action → info. The original files often had invented "monitor" or "caution" actions; these were corrected to info.

## Coordinator points: answers

- **Amlodipine/valsartan/HCTZ: confirmed no-uk-product.**
  - emc has 56 hydrochlorothiazide results. The only amlodipine triple among them is olmesartan/amlodipine/HCTZ.
  - The MHRA Products index has 0 SPC or PIL records that contain all three ingredients.
  - The file is in exact SCHEMA no-uk-product form.
- **Perindopril/amlodipine salt: acceptable.**
  - Perindopril tert-butylamine and perindopril arginine are salts of the same active moiety.
  - The UK SmPC states that 8 mg tert-butylamine is equivalent to 10 mg arginine. Perindopril content is 3.34 vs 3.395 mg, and 6.68 vs 6.79 mg.
  - The formulation note and dosing item map the clinic strengths: 5/5→4/5, 5/10→4/10, 10/5→8/5, 10/10→8/10. They warn against mg-for-mg swaps.
  - No UK arginine/amlodipine product exists. The tert-butylamine products are licensed.
- **Captopril tablets via MHRA SPC: confirmed.**
  - The Tillomed 25 mg and 50 mg SPCs are current (revised 14/05/2026) and match the clinic strengths.
  - The emc clinic link is the oral solution and is correctly not used.
- **Amlodipine/valsartan renal 30-60 inference: removed.**
  - It is now a `renal-impairment` condition rule (monitor), which quotes the moderate-impairment monitoring sentence.
  - The GFR ≤30 info rule is kept, because ≤30 is the complement of the SmPC's stated "GFR >30" no-adjustment range.
- **Group `diuretic`.**
  - It now holds sourced members: spironolactone (B); valsartan-HCTZ and telmisartan-HCTZ (K); lisinopril-HCTZ (L); indapamide, HCTZ, furosemide, co-amilozide and atenolol/chlortalidone (M).
  - All member quotes are FOUND.
  - Amlodipine-valsartan-HCTZ is not a member, because it is no-uk-product and so membership cannot be sourced.
  - Rules meaning only "thiazide or loop" or "non-potassium-sparing" diuretics were moved to `group:thiazide` + `group:loop-diuretic` (captopril, perindopril-amlodipine).
  - Rules whose source means all diuretics stay on `group:diuretic` (perindopril 4.2; amlodipine-valsartan 4.4).
- **HCTZ skin-cancer page:** one id, `mhra-dsu-hctz-nmsc-2018`, is now used in L, M and K (see 0.3).
- **Atenolol-bendroflumethiazide.json:**
  - Name, ingredients, className, labels, strengths and all text say atenolol/chlortalidone.
  - Both formulation notes explain the clinic label mismatch and ask the reader to confirm the pack. No UK atenolol + bendroflumethiazide product exists; the only UK bendroflumethiazide combination is with timolol.
  - The file id is left for the integrator.
  - The only remaining "bendroflumethiazide" strings are the file id (used in the allergy rule allergen, because `chlortalidone` is not a vocab ingredient) and one searchTerm, which lets a search for the clinic label find it.
