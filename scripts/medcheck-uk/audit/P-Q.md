# Audit: batches P and Q

Batch P: amitriptyline, mirtazapine, fluoxetine, escitalopram, rowatinex (terpene combination), cystone.
Batch Q: venlafaxine, desvenlafaxine, duloxetine, carbamazepine, betahistine, cinnarizine.

This is an independent second check, done on 2026-10-17. All 40 sources in `sources/P.json` and `sources/Q.json` were re-fetched. The following SmPC sections were read in full for every UK-sourced medicine: 4.1 to 4.6, plus the QT statements in 4.8 and 5.1. The SPS QT list was read in full. The MHRA products search index (the index behind products.mhra.gov.uk) and emc search were queried again for Rowatinex, Cystone, desvenlafaxine and cinnarizine.

After the edits, every quote in the 12 medicine files and in their group memberships was re-checked against the cached page text with `lib/text.mjs` `quoteFound`. The result was **1395 quotes checked, 0 not found**, and `validate()` reports 0 schema errors for these files. The Teva cinnarizine SPC was re-uploaded on MHRA on 16 Oct 2026, so it was re-fetched fresh. Its quotes are still found, and its revision date is unchanged (15/11/2021).

## 2. Coordinator questions, answered

- **Rowatinex licence.** The MHRA products search index lists "ROWATINEX CAPSULES", PL 00007/0003R, with SPC and PIL documents dated 2026-09-25. The six actives match (pinene, fenchone, camphene, borneol, cineole, anethole). emc has no entry. MHRA products only carries products on the current register, so the licence looks current. The SPC text itself is still the 2004 revision, and the PIL is from April 2009. Kept as a UK product. The PIL title line lists "Menthone, Menthol", but its section 6 and the SPC list fenchone and anethol. The SPC composition is used.
- **Cystone: confirmed no-uk-product.** emc search returns "No search results for cystone". The MHRA products index has 0 hits.
- **Desvenlafaxine: confirmed no-uk-product.** emc returns "No search results". The MHRA products index by product name ("desvenlafaxine", "pristiq") has 0 hits. Full-text hits are only incidental mentions in unrelated leaflets (a DaTSCAN and a methylthioninium chloride document). The file has `rules: []`, every section is not-available, and the formulation has no `src`.
- **Cinnarizine 25 mg.** The MHRA products index lists only cinnarizine 15 mg tablets (three SPCs) and cinnarizine/dimenhydrinate 20 mg/40 mg. The clinic-map link emc 15869 is the 20/40 combination, and it is correctly **not** used. The 15 mg SmPCs are used, and the formulation note says so and tells the user to convert doses. Doses in the data are written as numbers of 15 mg tablets.
- **QT policy.** In the group: amitriptyline (SPS TCA class, plus SmPC 4.8 "common QT prolonged") and escitalopram (SPS and SmPC: dose-dependent). Out of the group: fluoxetine (SPS: "no effect on QTc at licensed doses"), mirtazapine (SmPC 5.1: below the threshold for clinical meaning), duloxetine (SmPC: no QTc difference from placebo), cinnarizine (SmPC: no QTc prolongation in vivo) and carbamazepine (no QT statement). Venlafaxine is now removed (see section 1). Each excluded medicine keeps its own caution or avoid rule toward `group:qt-prolonging` where its SmPC gives one.
- **Serotonergic / SSRI / SNRI memberships.** All sourced from each medicine's own SmPC: SSRI for fluoxetine and escitalopram; SNRI for venlafaxine and duloxetine; serotonergic for all six antidepressants. Desvenlafaxine has no memberships, because it has no UK product.
- **Carbamazepine with prednisolone/dexamethasone.** `other` is the exact id, and both ids are oral-only products (prednisolone tabs/solution, dexamethasone oral solutions). The ear and eye steroid combinations are matched only by ingredient, and the engine ignores ingredient matches for local routes. No change needed. The ciprofloxacin rules (carbamazepine and duloxetine) use `otherFormulations` `ciprofloxacin-tab` and `ciprofloxacin-susp`, which are the only ciprofloxacin formulation ids.
- **MAOI rules.** Rules toward `group:maoi` exist for amitriptyline, mirtazapine, fluoxetine, escitalopram, venlafaxine, duloxetine and carbamazepine (all contraindicated) and betahistine (caution). The group is empty, so they never fire. They are kept for when an MAOI is added.
- **Elderly 65-year assumption.** Where the SmPC says only "elderly", the concern now states that 65 is used as the trigger: venlafaxine, duloxetine, fluoxetine (new) and mirtazapine (new). The other SmPCs give the age themselves: amitriptyline "over 65", escitalopram "> 65", carbamazepine "65 years or above". Note that `minYears: 65` also includes people aged exactly 65 where the source says "over 65". This is a minor widening, and it was left as is.

## 3. Problems not fixed (for other batches or the integrator)

1. **Batch D: sertraline is in `qt-prolonging`, but SPS says "Fluoxetine, paroxetine and sertraline have no effect on QTc at licensed doses".** Under the QT policy it should be removed. As things stand, escitalopram + sertraline is shown as **contraindicated** through escitalopram's QT rule. That pair is already flagged through the serotonergic route. The QT bases for diltiazem (N), azithromycin (AA) and metoclopramide (W) were not checked here and should be confirmed against the same policy.
2. **Inhaled antimuscarinics are in `anticholinergic`** (tiotropium, ipratropium, fluticasone furoate/umeclidinium/vilanterol). Amitriptyline's "avoid" rule (SmPC: "Anticholinergic agents … concomitant use of these should be avoided") therefore fires against inhalers. The SmPC does not tell routes apart. The integrator should decide whether inhaled products belong in the systemic group.
3. **Engine `isLocal` limits.** Amitriptyline's SmPC names "nasal decongestants" explicitly, but xylometazoline nasal spray is ignored for the `group:sympathomimetic` rule. Mirtazapine's "azole antifungals" rule is likewise ignored for miconazole (oromucosal gel). Both are by design. Note them if exact-id rules are wanted.
4. **Batch T:** paracetamol-pseudoephedrine-chlorphenamine(+caffeine) are `no-uk-product` but are still listed as members of the `antihistamine` and `sympathomimetic` groups in T.json. As a result, other medicines' rules (betahistine, amitriptyline) fire against an unsourced product. Also, `sedating-antihistamine` contains only cinnarizine.
5. **Batch C:** `cyp2c19-inhibitor` lists ciprofloxacin. This was not checked here, but it makes escitalopram's CYP2C19 dose rule fire for ciprofloxacin. Please confirm the source.
6. **Carbamazepine trigeminal neuralgia.** The SmPC is internally inconsistent: "doses of 1600mg … may be needed" vs "Maximum recommended dose is 1200mg/day". Both are stated in the item. Left as it is.
7. **Rowatinex** has only a 2004 SPC and a 2009 PIL. Its UK information is old, and there is no hepatic or renal guidance.

## 4. Per-medicine assessment

- **Amitriptyline: good.** 4.3 is fully encoded (MI, heart block, rhythm disorder, coronary insufficiency, severe liver disease, under 6 years, MAOI). The 4.5 levels follow the SmPC headings: QT drugs, tramadol, fluconazole, sympathomimetics and methyldopa are under "not recommended", so they are `avoid`; CNS depressants are under "requiring precautions", so they are `caution`. Strong CYP2D6 inhibitors map to fluoxetine at dose level, and duloxetine is caution. Pregnancy and breastfeeding conflicts (SmPC vs BUMPS/SPS) are kept separately. Non-master interactions (thioridazine, valproate, cimetidine) are in the text only.
- **Mirtazapine: good.** 4.3 is complete. The renal threshold (CrCl <40, exclusive) and the hepatic caution are correct. The serotonergic, sedative, warfarin INR, QT and CYP3A4 rules match 4.5. An elderly rule was added.
- **Fluoxetine: good.** The correct product (the 20 mg capsule SmPC) is used, and the dispersible-tablet SmPC is correctly rejected. The MAOI contraindication is for the irreversible non-selective type, and the concern says so. Metoprolol and tamoxifen are not in the master list. Bleeding, QT, TCA/carbamazepine dose and hyponatraemia rules are all present. An elderly dose rule was added.
- **Escitalopram: good.** The 4.3 QT contraindications (condition plus interaction) are encoded as contraindicated. The CYP2C19 dose rule, the elderly and hepatic 10 mg limits and the renal CLCR <30 caution are correct. The SmPC (breastfeeding not recommended) vs SPS (use with caution) conflict is kept as two rules.
- **Rowatinex (terpene combination): acceptable.** It is on the current UK register, but the SPC is from 2004. Every SPC statement is encoded. The allergy rule comes from the PIL, because SPC 4.3 says "None known". The UTI rule wording was corrected.
- **Cystone: correct as no-uk-product.**
- **Venlafaxine: good after the fixes.** 4.3 is complete. The renal bands (GFR 30–70 inclusive caution; <30 halve the dose) and hepatic bands are correct. The QT membership was removed, the opioid rule narrowed and the insulin duplicate deleted. Multiple overlapping serotonergic rules (SSRI/SNRI/TCA/serotonergic) merge in the UI by effect tag.
- **Desvenlafaxine: correct as no-uk-product.**
- **Duloxetine: good.** All six 4.3 items are encoded at contraindicated, including ciprofloxacin restricted to its systemic forms, CrCl <30, any hepatic impairment, and uncontrolled hypertension (now qualified as "do not start"). The SmPC (breastfeeding not recommended) vs SPS conflict is kept.
- **Carbamazepine: good.** 4.3 is complete (including TCA cross-hypersensitivity and porphyria cutanea tarda). Every master-list substance in 4.5 has a rule: macrolides, ciprofloxacin, fluoxetine, fluconazole, CYP3A4 inhibitors, omeprazole, St John's wort, paracetamol, tramadol, doxycycline, warfarin, sertraline, TCAs, progestogens, dihydropyridines, simvastatin, atorvastatin, prednisolone, dexamethasone, levothyroxine, HCTZ, furosemide, diuretics and metoclopramide. HLA-B*1502 is at avoid; HLA-A*3101 is at caution. Women of childbearing potential and contraception are covered. Brand prescribing is set to category 1.
- **Betahistine: good.** A small SmPC, fully encoded. The quote fix is minor.
- **Cinnarizine: acceptable.** The strength gap (25 mg clinic vs 15 mg UK) is stated clearly. The porphyria conflict is kept: Teva 4.3 says contraindicated, and the brand/Viatris 4.4 say avoid. The brand SmPC covers motion sickness only. The vestibular indication and its dose come from the generic SPCs.
